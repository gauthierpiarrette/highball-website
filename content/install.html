Install Highball

Highball is a free, open-source Mac app. It is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens without any warnings when you download it the normal way.

Requirements

  • Apple Silicon (M1 or newer). Intel Macs are not supported.
  • macOS 14 Sonoma or newer.
  • Around 8 GB of free space for the engine and the Windows environment, plus whatever your games need.
  • Rosetta 2, which macOS installs for you the first time it is needed.

Download

Download Highball.dmg

Open the disk image, drag Highball to Applications, and launch it. That is the whole installation. The app updates itself from then on.

First launch

Press Get started. Highball installs Rosetta if your Mac lacks it, downloads the Wine engine (a few minutes on a normal connection) and prepares one Windows environment for your games. The engine is assembled from pinned upstream releases, each one checked against a recorded SHA-256, so you get exactly the build that was tested. Then the library asks where your games are: install Steam, connect Epic, or pick a Windows program you already have. Your games appear with a compatibility verdict on each one, and every game's page says what Play will do before you press it.

Environments, the engine and troubleshooting live in Settings (⌘,). You never need them to play a game.

Uninstalling

Drag the app to the Trash. Everything else lives in ~/Library/Application Support/Highball, which holds the engine, your Windows environments and your games. Delete that folder too if you want the disk space back, but note that your installed games live inside it.
