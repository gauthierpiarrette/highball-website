Help get Forza Horizon 6's stutter fixed

Forza Horizon 6 freezes for about a tenth of a second roughly once a second, whatever the settings, on every Mac we have heard from. In Apple's Metal HUD it shows as tall spikes in the frame time graph. The cause is in Rosetta, the part of macOS that runs Intel code on Apple silicon, so neither Highball nor Wine can fix it. Apple can, and every report that points at the same problem makes it more likely they will.

Five minutes, nothing to install, no Terminal. Open Feedback Assistant, paste the two texts below, and press Submit.

What is going on

About once a second the game's copy protection runs a check while the game waits for it. The check triggers about 2,000 CPU exceptions on purpose and handles each one itself. On a PC an exception takes one or two microseconds, so the whole check is over in a few milliseconds. Under Rosetta each one takes 15 to 60 microseconds, so the check takes about a tenth of a second, and that is the hitch you see.

We measured it on an M4 Mac mini with small test programs. The same exception costs about 1.5 microseconds in native Apple silicon code, about 15 under Rosetta, and about 32 under Rosetta when other threads are busy, as they always are in a game. Wine's own share is about 5%. We tried every setting we could find, real-time priority included, and the best of them shortens the hitch by about a fifth. Any game whose protection works this way is hit the same way. Forza Horizon 6 is the one we measured.

Why your report helps

We sent Apple a detailed report with the test programs and every measurement. Its number is FB_NUMBER. Apple weighs how many people a problem affects when it decides what to fix, and a report that names that number gets linked to it. You do not need to be a developer or run anything.

Send it

  1. Open Feedback Assistant. It is already on your Mac, so press ⌘ Space and type its name, or use feedbackassistant.apple.com. Sign in with your Apple Account.
  2. Press New Feedback and choose macOS.
  3. For the area, choose AREA_NAME. If it is not in the list, choose Something else not on this list.
  4. For the kind of problem, choose TYPE_NAME.
  5. Copy the title and the description below into the form, and fill in your Mac and macOS version.
  6. If it offers to collect diagnostics, let it, ideally while Forza is running and stuttering. They go to Apple only. A short screen recording of the hitch with the Metal HUD on helps too.
  7. Press Submit. If you like, post your FB number in this thread, so we can show Apple how many players it affects.
